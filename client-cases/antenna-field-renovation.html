Antenna Field Upgrade Project

Customer Cases / Upgrade Projects
Project Overview

Our engineering team undertook an upgrade project for an antenna field, which involved dismantling and replacing one existing shortwave antenna and installing two additional shortwave antenna systems. The project passed the superior authority’s acceptance inspection in a single review and effectively improved the shortwave communication capability of the unit.
