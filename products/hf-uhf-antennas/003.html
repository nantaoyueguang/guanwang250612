Shortwave Three-wire Inverted V Antenna

Product Overview

　　The Shortwave Three-Wire Inverted V Antenna features a wide operating frequency, no antenna tuning required, horizontal-plane omnidirectional radiation, simple structure, reliable performance, and easy and quick deployment. It enables medium- to short-range communication without blind spots and is an ideal choice for shortwave station communications.

Technical Specifications

Parameter Name Parameter Value
Frequency Range 3～30MHz
Rated Power 2KW
